How to Play Pente

Board & Opening: Played on a 19×19 grid. Stones sit on line intersections (including edges). The first player opens on the center point.

Two Ways to Win:

  • 5-in-a-Row: Connect 5 or more consecutive stones horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. (6+ also wins immediately and cannot be broken by capture.)
  • 5 Captures: Capture 5 pairs (10 stones) of your opponent's stones.

Captures: Bracket exactly two adjacent enemy stones with your stones in a straight line (X O O X) to remove them. Multiple pairs can be captured in one move.

No Self-Capture: Playing into an empty gap between two enemy stones to form a pair (O X X O) is safe—your stones are not captured.

Mobile Tip: Touch and drag on the board to aim with the crosshair loupe, or tap 🔍 to zoom in. Enable Tap-to-Confirm in Setup for extra precision.
