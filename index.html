How to Play Pente

Objective: Win by placing 5 or more beads in an unbroken line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal), or by capturing 5 pairs (10 beads) of your opponent's stones.

Opening: The first player places the opening bead on the center point (J10). Players then alternate placing one bead per turn on any open intersection.

Captures: Trap exactly two adjacent opponent beads between two of your own along any line (X · O · O · X) to remove them from the board. Multiple pairs can be captured in a single move.
